# XAUUSD Research Plan

## Purpose
Discover durable conditional edges rather than force one always-on strategy.

## Research families
Investigate independently and in controlled combinations: regime-conditioned trend/continuation; liquidity/structure behavior; momentum persistence/exhaustion; volatility compression/expansion; conditional mean reversion; session/opening behavior; multi-timeframe context; explicit abstention/no-trade conditions.

Historical EMA, SMC, FVG, ORB, ADX and momentum systems may serve as baselines/hypothesis sources only.

## Research sequence
1. Approve data and execution assumptions.
2. Establish simple baselines.
3. Generate one falsifiable hypothesis per experiment.
4. Test aggregate and conditional behavior.
5. Identify failure clusters.
6. Stress robustness before combining agents.
7. Combine only complementary, independently useful evidence.
8. Calibrate opportunity confidence.
9. Hand candidate opportunity to Risk Governor; never bypass it.

## Key questions
- In which regimes does the edge exist/disappear?
- Is profitability concentrated in a tiny period/session?
- Does realistic spread/slippage erase it?
- Does it survive small parameter changes?
- Is the edge dependent on a particular data vendor?
- What conditions should produce NO TRADE?
- Are specialist agents adding independent information or voting on the same feature?

## Output
A small portfolio of validated conditional behaviors is preferable to a complex universal rule set.
