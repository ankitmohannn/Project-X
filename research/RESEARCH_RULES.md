# Research Rules

1. Begin with a falsifiable market hypothesis, not an indicator combination.
2. Freeze data/assumptions before comparing candidates.
3. Prevent lookahead/repainting and timestamp leakage.
4. Model realistic costs and execution constraints.
5. Record no-trade decisions where relevant.
6. Segment results by regime/session/volatility, not just aggregate metrics.
7. Prefer stable parameter regions and simple causal rules.
8. Treat post-hoc fixes as new experiments.
9. Preserve failures and negative results.
10. Do not let an LLM/agent invent or reinterpret missing data.
11. Compare new ideas against simple baselines and the cost of doing nothing.
12. Seek robustness across changing conditions rather than a universal always-on strategy.
13. Use confidence only after calibration; never translate arbitrary LLM confidence directly into lot size.
14. Promotion requires the validation gates in `docs/VALIDATION_FRAMEWORK.md`.
